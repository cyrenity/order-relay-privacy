Order Relay privacy information

Order Relay is a private order-management tool operated by the connected store administrator. It reads orders from authorized eBay accounts on request and exports them to a Google spreadsheet selected by that administrator.

It stores encrypted seller authorization tokens, order details, buyer and shipping information, export progress, and keyed hashes of account identifiers used for account-deletion matching. Vercel hosts the application, Neon stores its database, and Google hosts the destination spreadsheet. Access is restricted to the administrator and the service accounts configured for this application.

Verified eBay account-deletion notifications remove matching data from the active application database and app-managed spreadsheet cells, revoke the stored connection for a deleted seller, and prevent the same account data from being imported again. Failed cleanup is retried through eBay notification delivery. The administrator must manage independent copies, backups, spreadsheet revision history, and any required retention separately.

Only the authorized store administrator can connect accounts or choose the export destination. For access, privacy, or data-removal questions, contact the business operating the eBay store associated with your order.
